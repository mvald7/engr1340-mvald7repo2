# Subtraction of Integers

Subtraction is the process of finding the difference between two integers. Subtracting an integer is equivalent to adding its opposite.

## Example 1

* First integer: 10
* Second integer: 4
* Calculation: 10 - 4 = 6

The difference between 10 and 4 is 6.

## Example 2

* First integer: -3
* Second integer: 5
* Calculation: -3 - 5 = -8

The difference between -3 and 5 is -8.
